## Defect severity
- **P0:** security/auth bypass, wrong-customer access, corrupt balance/ledger, duplicate money movement, hidden financial record, irreversible destructive action.
- **P1:** customer-impacting function fails, wrong status/math, required email fails, staff/customer views disagree.
- **P2:** workflow works but messaging, validation, navigation or UX is materially confusing.
- **P3:** cosmetic/non-blocking issue.
